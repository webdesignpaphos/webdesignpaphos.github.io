If you've searched Google recently, you've noticed something different. Before any list of websites, there's now a block of AI-generated text answering your question directly. This is Google's AI Overview — and it is fundamentally changing how customers find local businesses in Paphos, across Cyprus, and worldwide.

The old game was simple: rank on page one, get clicks. The new game is harder to see but just as important: make sure the AI mentions your business, or at least sends people to your website when they want to know more.

## What AI Search Means for a Business in Paphos

When someone in Limassol types "best restaurant in Paphos" or "web design agency Paphos Cyprus," Google's AI now often gives a summarised answer before showing any links. If your business is well-represented online — clear information, good reviews, consistent details — there's a real chance the AI references you or your website. If your online presence is thin or inconsistent, you're invisible in that AI block.

This isn't theoretical. Studies from early 2026 show that AI Overviews appear in roughly 30 percent of all searches in English-language markets. For local searches — the kind a tourist looking for a boat trip or a Paphos resident searching for a plumber would do — that percentage is even higher.

## Answer Engine Optimisation: The New Layer of SEO

Alongside traditional SEO, a new term has emerged: Answer Engine Optimisation, or AEO. The idea is straightforward — structure your website content so that AI systems (Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity) can easily extract accurate answers and cite your business.

For a Paphos business, that means:

**Write in questions and answers.** Add an FAQ section to your website. Not generic questions, but specific ones your customers actually ask. "Do you offer web design services in Paphos?" "What is the average cost of a website in Cyprus?" "How long does it take to build a local business website?" Each question-and-answer pair gives AI systems a clear, citable piece of information.

**Use structured data markup.** This is code added to your website that explicitly labels your business name, address, phone number, opening hours, and services. It's invisible to visitors but highly readable by AI. A web developer can add this in an afternoon, and the long-term benefit is substantial.

**Keep your Google Business Profile updated.** Your Google Business Profile is one of the primary sources AI Overview draws from for local businesses. Your business name, address, phone number, and hours must match exactly across your website, Google, and every directory listing. Even a small inconsistency — an old phone number on an outdated directory — can reduce your AI visibility.

## Core Web Vitals Still Matter Enormously

While AI search is the new topic, the fundamentals haven't gone away. Google's Core Web Vitals — the set of metrics measuring how fast and stable your website feels to a visitor — remain a significant ranking factor in 2026.

The three metrics that matter most:

**Largest Contentful Paint (LCP):** How quickly the main content of your page loads. Under 2.5 seconds is good. Over 4 seconds is poor and actively hurts rankings.

**Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS):** Whether your page jumps around as it loads. Nothing frustrates a mobile user more than trying to tap a button that moves just as they touch it.

**Interaction to Next Paint (INP):** How quickly your page responds when a visitor clicks or taps something. This metric replaced First Input Delay in 2024 and is now one of the core signals.

You can check your own scores for free at [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/). Enter your website URL and you'll get a detailed breakdown of where you stand.

## Mobile Is Not Optional — It Is the Default

More than 70 percent of local searches in Cyprus happen on a smartphone. If your website is not designed primarily for mobile — fast loading, easy to navigate with a thumb, text large enough to read in sunlight — you are losing customers every day.

Mobile-first design means the mobile version of your website is built first, and the desktop version adapts from there. It also means:

- Phone numbers should be clickable to call directly
- Forms should be short and easy to fill in on a small screen
- Images should be compressed so they load quickly on mobile networks
- Navigation menus should work with a single tap, not require hovering

## What to Do This Month

If you own a business in Paphos and want to stay visible as search continues to evolve, here are the most impactful actions you can take right now:

1. **Audit your Google Business Profile.** Log in and confirm every detail is current and accurate. Add recent photos if you haven't in the last three months.

2. **Check your website speed on mobile.** Use PageSpeed Insights. If your mobile score is below 50, treat it as urgent.

3. **Add an FAQ section to your main service page.** Five to ten real questions your customers ask, with clear answers. This single addition has an outsized impact on both AI visibility and traditional SEO.

4. **Search for your business on Google.** Type your business name plus "Paphos." Check that your address, phone, and hours are correct in every result. Flag any outdated listings for correction.

5. **Make your phone number prominent.** It should be visible without scrolling on your homepage, on mobile, with one tap to call.

The businesses in Paphos that will win local search over the next few years are not necessarily the biggest ones. They are the ones with the clearest, most accurate, most useful online presence. That is a game every business can compete in.
